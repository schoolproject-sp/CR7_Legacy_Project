Captain & Icon of A Seleção

Since making his senior international debut at age 18 in August 2003, Ronaldo has transformed Portugal into a perennial European powerhouse and holds the world record for both men's international appearances and goals.

"Winning the European Championship for Portugal in 2016 was the greatest moment of my career."

Historic International Moments

  • UEFA Euro 2016 Champions: Captained Portugal to their first-ever major international trophy in Paris, motivating his teammates from the touchline after an early final injury.
  • 2019 UEFA Nations League: Scored a semi-final hat-trick against Switzerland to lift Portugal's second major international title on home soil in Porto.
  • 5 Consecutive World Cups (2006–2022): The first male player in history to score in five different FIFA World Cup tournaments.
