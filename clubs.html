Conquering Domestic Leagues Across Four Nations

Cristiano Ronaldo is the first footballer in history to win league titles, domestic cups, and super cups across England, Spain, and Italy before continuing his scoring streak in Saudi Arabia.

Defining Chapters of His Club Journey

  • Manchester United (2003–2009 & 2021–2022): Inherited the iconic No. 7 shirt, won three consecutive Premier League titles, and captured his first Champions League and Ballon d'Or in 2008.
  • Real Madrid (2009–2018): Became the club's all-time leading scorer with more than a goal per game, spearheading four UEFA Champions League triumphs in five years.
  • Juventus (2018–2021): Won back-to-back Serie A titles and became the fastest player in Juventus history to reach 100 official goals.
  • Al-Nassr (2023–Present): Set the single-season Saudi Pro League scoring record with 35 league goals in 2023–24.
