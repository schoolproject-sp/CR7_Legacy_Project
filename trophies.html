The CR7 Trophy Cabinet

From individual world player awards to continental club championships, Ronaldo's trophy collection spans over twenty seasons of elite competition.

Major Team & Individual Honors

  • 5x Ballon d'Or: Awarded in 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, and 2017 as the world's best footballer.
  • 5x UEFA Champions League: Won once with Manchester United (2008) and four times with Real Madrid (2014, 2016, 2017, 2018).
  • 7x Domestic League Titles: 3 Premier Leagues, 2 La Liga titles, and 2 Serie A championships.
  • 4x FIFA Club World Cup: Crowned world club champion in 2008, 2014, 2016, and 2017.
  • UEFA Euro Golden Boot (2020) & FIFA Puskás Award (2009): Recognized for both tournament scoring and his 40-yard strike against FC Porto.
